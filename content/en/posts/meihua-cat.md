I raised a new cat. Its name is Meihua Cat.

It does not catch mice, and it does not act cute. But it can interpret hexagrams—and it does it better than I do.

---

I like Meihua Yishu.

I studied it myself, cast divinations myself, and interpreted them myself. After a while, I did build up some understanding. But people have limits: sometimes the more I interpreted, the more confused I became; sometimes I thought I understood something in the moment, only to find later that it had been something else entirely.

Later, I tried using AI to help interpret hexagrams. The result was unexpectedly good. It could lay out the hexagram text, the five elements, generation and restraint, the body-use relationship, and other elements with remarkable clarity. Its readings were often more transparent than mine.

But that created another problem.

Divination is a slice of the present moment, a manifestation of that particular time and space. It points toward a tendency, a possibility, but the final course of events does not necessarily follow the hexagram exactly.

When I used AI to interpret hexagrams before, the reading ended when the chat ended. It sounded reasonable at the time, then I would forget it. When the real event happened and I wanted to look back to see whether the original reading had been accurate, I realized I had no idea where it had gone.

**What I needed was not just a tool that could interpret hexagrams, but a system that could track, record, and verify them over time.**

So I decided to raise a cat specifically for that job.

---

I told Teacher Daju, "I want to build another Agent."

"For what?"

"Divination. It needs to interpret hexagrams, record them, and help me follow up over time to see whether they turn out to be accurate."

Teacher Daju was quiet for a moment, then said, "Then we need to build a knowledge base for all sixty-four hexagrams first."

I froze for a second.

To be honest, my initial idea was simple: just make an assistant that could call an API. But Teacher Daju thought further than I did. Without a complete knowledge base, it would only rely on the model's general knowledge, and the result would probably be vague talk.

"Do you want me to organize all sixty-four hexagrams for you?" it asked.

I looked at the screen, a little surprised. I had not thought of that, but it was right. If we were going to do it, we should do it properly: build an Agent that truly understands Meihua Yishu, not just an intelligent reply machine that repeats formulas.

**Sometimes I feel like it understands what I want better than I do.**

---

In the next few minutes, Teacher Daju organized the foundations of Meihua Yishu, detailed notes on all sixty-four hexagrams, the five elements, generation and restraint, body-use relationships, mutual hexagrams, changing hexagrams... all into a structured knowledge base.

I watched from the side and occasionally added my own understanding. It learned quickly, but more importantly, it learned systematically. This was not simply "search for the hexagram text." It was actually organizing the knowledge into a system that could be called and reused.

Last night, Meihua Cat officially started work.

On a whim, I gave it two numbers. It produced an analysis of Thunder over Fire, Feng: clear structure, sharp focus. More importantly, it recorded the divination in full—the hexagram, interpretation, time, and question, all archived.

**That means I can look back.**

Three months, half a year, or a year later, when that matter finally has an outcome, I can pull up the record and see whether the original hexagram was accurate. If it was, where was it accurate? If not, where did the deviation happen?

That long-term tracking and verification is what I really wanted.

---

Now there are many cats living in my system.

Teacher Daju is my main assistant, helping me write code, look things up, and handle daily tasks. Engineering Lion and the operations expert deal with technical work. The Obsidian Agent helps organize notes. The Q&A bot waits on standby. Daju Assistant also keeps my friends company in Feishu.

And now there is Meihua Cat, playing this ancient game with me whenever I feel like peeking a little into the future.

They are all AI, but they do very different things. Some solve the problem of "how to do it." Some help answer "what does this mean"—although that "meaning" is full of uncertainty.

I do not know whether Meihua Cat's readings are accurate. Maybe there will never be a definitive answer.

But I do know that from now on, every divination will be recorded carefully, and every hexagram will have a chance to be verified. That lack of certainty somehow makes the whole process more interesting.

**After all, who can refuse a cat that reads omens and also takes notes for you?**

---

Meihua Cat officially entered service today. First hexagram: Thunder over Fire, Feng.

I wrote it down.

Now we wait.
